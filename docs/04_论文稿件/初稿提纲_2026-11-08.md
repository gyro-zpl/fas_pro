## 0. 定位

**建议标题**（择一）：

1. *From Prompting to Structured Alignment: A Data-Centric Pipeline for LLM Survey Simulation*
2. *When Structured Survey Data Helps: Individual, Population, and Temporal Boundaries of LLM Social Simulation*
3. *Beyond Prompting: Fine-Tuning Compact LLMs for Individual and Population-Level Survey Simulation*

**核心主张**：

- **C1（个体级）**：结构化调查数据微调让 8B 模型在**同分布、陌生人**条件下获得真实个体信号（MAE 20.8、corr 0.68、choice 0.54 vs chance 0.16），与强 GBM 持平。
- **C2（群体级）**：FAS 微调显著降低群体分布差异（EDC 边际 JS −93%、联合 −86%）。
- **C3（时间边界）**：4 年时间偏移下 LLM 与 GBM 都失去个体信号（corr 0.72 → −0.06）；LLM 衰减更早。这是本文最重要的分析结果。

**不要写**：
- "LLM 显著优于 GBM"（不支持）。
- "2024 上 MAE 下降 = 提升"（corr ≈ 0，塌缩）。
- "所有领域都适用"（只做了 ANES + GSS）。

## 1. 摘要草稿（约 180 词）

> LLMs are increasingly used as virtual survey respondents, but most evaluations treat surveys as one-shot prompting tasks. We present a data-centric pipeline that reconstructs voter–candidate and voter–group interactions from ANES, aligns a compact 8B model with survey distributions via LoRA, and evaluates both individual-level prediction and population-level simulation. On unseen respondents in the same era, the fine-tuned model reaches MAE 20.8, correlation 0.68, and choice accuracy 0.54 (chance 0.16), competitive with a strong gradient-boosted baseline. On GSS policy attitudes, fine-tuning reduces marginal and joint Jensen–Shannon divergence by 93% and 86% on the EDC configuration. We then show a sharp temporal boundary: across the 2019→2024 waves, individual-level correlation decays from 0.72 to near zero, and both the LLM and the tabular baseline collapse in 2024. Our results suggest that structured alignment is necessary but not sufficient for social simulation, and that temporal distribution shift should be treated as a first-class evaluation axis.
